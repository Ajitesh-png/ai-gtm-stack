# X Content Playbook — performance-grounded rules

Source: full-population teardown of the founder's account, 1 Jul – 31 Aug 2026. 11 original
posts, ranked by views / comments (replies) / saves (bookmarks). Median reach
~465K; outlier floor 914. This is measured data, not theory — when a rule below
conflicts with intuition, the rule wins until re-measured.

**Refresh rule:** this snapshot ages. Re-run the same teardown (X date-filtered
search, all originals, views/comments/saves) at the start of each quarter and
update the numbers and rules here. Rules derived from Jul–Aug 2026 hold until then.

---

## RULE 0 — pick the target metric BEFORE writing

The single most reusable finding: **the three metrics have three different
levers, and they are nearly independent.**

| You want | The lever | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Reach (views)** | news peg + category-killer line, or a top-4 hook | top 3 posts all ride a launch or a labour reframe (1.88M / 1.35M / 1.32M) |
| **Replies (leads)** | a "Comment [WORD]" gate — replies are *engineered, not earned* | remove the gate and replies collapse to single/low-double digits **even at 1M+ views** (Aug 20/24/26) |
| **Saves (intent)** | teach a repeatable, portable move — a named concept or playbook | saves cluster on "white space" (147), the 48x playbook (93), the launch (1,581) |

A post that doesn't know which of the three it's chasing will get none of them.
Decide first; the shape follows.

## RULE 1 — the hook does ~90% of the work

Every winner opens with one of **four hook families**. The body rarely changes
the outcome; the first line does.

1. **Bold reframe / category kill** — "Your whole ad team is now a single hire."
   · "The report era of AI just ended."
2. **Metaphor / curiosity gap** — "Your best salesperson sells from a room
   nobody visits."
3. **In-scene story or dialogue** — open mid-scene on a Slack ping ("…Found a
   4th nobody's touched. Build it?") or a founder moment ("woke up to
   notifications stacked on her lock screen").
4. **Contrarian reversal** — "max talked them out of showing skin at all." ·
   "their own product never appears in it." **Strongest family for cheap reach
   in the dataset** — lead every teardown with it.

A flat factual announcement is quiet. The announcement **welded to a one-line
worldview** is what travels.

## RULE 2 — the copy skeleton (repeatable)

1. **Line 1:** the hook (one of the four families) — carries the scroll-stop.
2. White space, then a **1–2 line problem or contrast, with a number** —
   347 ads, $18 CPA, 3.2×, 48x, 11 bottles. **A concrete figure in the first
   three lines buys credibility before the claim.** (Numbers must be real or
   clearly illustrative — never invented performance figures; run-times,
   counts and structural numbers are the safe kind.)
3. A scannable **→ bullet list** — what the agent did or understood. Never
   paragraphs.
4. A **provocative reframe closer** — often a signature refrain.
5. **CTA only if the goal is replies/leads** (Rule 0). Omit on pure-reach
   teardowns — but see Rule 5.

## RULE 3 — the product is a character

"Max" + the pronoun **"he"** recur across the best posts. The persona is the
account's single most differentiated asset: hireable-teammate framing, Slack
pings, day-in-the-life scenes. Personified SaaS is a story; features are not.
(Consistent with the locked hire-voice: he/him after the reveal, hire verbs,
never "it generated".)

## RULE 4 — signature refrains compound

"Marketers used to be people. Your next one won't be." reliably earns reposts
and saves across multiple posts. **Build a bank of refrains** so reposts have
more than one recognisable line to latch onto. A refrain is a portable closer:
short, quotable, slightly uncomfortable, and repeated verbatim across posts.

Current bank:
- "Marketers used to be people. Your next one won't be."
- "You sleep. Max ships."
- Candidates should be added here as they prove out (a refrain "proves out"
  when it appears in quote-tweets without prompting).

## RULE 5 — the CTA decision table

- **Launch / persona / integration posts:** comment-gate + a real offer
  ("Comment MAX — first 50 brands get…"). Drove 193 and 68 replies.
- **"Hidden money in your account" posts:** always gate — personal + gate is
  the highest reply-rate combo (Aug 25: 66 replies at 318K views).
- **Teardown teasers:** historically ungated and it cost us — Aug 20/24/26
  pulled 200K–1M views with single-digit replies. **Add "Comment TEARDOWN and
  I'll send the full breakdown"** — converts lurkers at no cost to the hook.
- Every gate word must have its **payload built before the post runs**, and
  each post gets a **distinct word** so replies are attributable.

## RULE 6 — the angle families in rotation

Rotate; never run one family two posts straight.

| Family | Posts | What it buys |
|---|---|---|
| **Launch & integration** (news-pegged) | Aug 5, Jul 22, Jul 30, Jul 9 | biggest reach + saves |
| **Customer creative teardown** (curiosity teaser → thread) | Aug 28, 26, 24, 20 | high views, low replies unless gated |
| **Hidden money in your account** (personal + gate) | Aug 25, Jul 1 | replies/leads |

## RULE 7 — the outlier rule (what never runs standalone)

Jul 11 ("the most mission-critical feature… is Ad previews") collapsed to
**914 views vs a ~465K median**. It was a founder-philosophy essay: no customer
outcome, no visual, no persona, no "you" benefit up top, no CTA.

**The lesson: on this account, craft does not buy reach.** Customer outcomes,
bold reframes and a visual do. Founder-philosophy content is allowed only:
- reformatted behind an outcome hook + a picture, or
- run as a **reply under a performing post**, never standalone.

## RULE 8 — news pegs are the cheapest reach

Ship a launch-style post **within 24h of every major AI-platform moment**
(Claude and ChatGPT news were the two largest reach events of the period).
Borrowed relevance beats owned cleverness. This pairs with the news-reactive
article lane — the X post is the fast twin of the Friday article.

## RULE 9 — cadence

One post every ~5 days worked. The two biggest gaps (mid-July, mid-August) sat
right before the account's best months — **more at-bats at this hit rate is
pure upside.** When planning a calendar, treat a >6-day gap as a defect.

## Pre-publish checklist (run on every draft)

- [ ] Target metric chosen (reach / replies / saves) and the shape matches it
- [ ] Hook is one of the four families; the first line survives without the rest
- [ ] A concrete number in the first three lines (real or clearly illustrative)
- [ ] → bullets, no paragraphs; white-space-heavy
- [ ] Max is "he", framed as a teammate, if the product appears
- [ ] Closer is a refrain or a reframe, not a summary
- [ ] CTA: gate word present iff goal = replies; word is unique; payload exists
- [ ] Not a standalone founder-philosophy essay (Rule 7)
- [ ] No invented performance numbers (permission + 2.5–4× range for real ones)
- [ ] ≤5 days since the last post, or this one closes the gap

## Calendar-planning rules (when planning a week/fortnight)

- Assign each slot a target metric first (Rule 0), then pick the concept.
- Rotate the three angle families (Rule 6); never two of one family in a row.
- Reserve capacity for one unplanned news-peg post per week (Rule 8).
- Teardowns get gates now (Rule 5) — plan the payload as part of the slot.
- Grounded concepts beat invented ones in every selection round to date —
  source from real sessions (`Watch Max Work`), real ads, real data.
