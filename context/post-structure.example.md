# Post structure (example)

Copy to `context/post-structure.md`. Read by `content-routine` and `launch-post`
before any X post is written. This is the loop law; the surface format lives
in `context/post-format.md`.

## The Zeigarnik loop

People finish what they started. A post that opens a loop in line one and
refuses to close it until the CTA gets read to the end. Three sentences, three
jobs:

| Sentence | Job | Test |
|---|---|---|
| **S1 opens** | specificity + a gap the brain cannot close | can the reader predict S2? if yes, rewrite |
| **S2 widens** | adds scale, stakes, or a mundane detail; does **not** resolve | did you explain the mechanism? if yes, you closed the loop too early |
| **S3 kills the easy explanation** | a take that re-diagnoses something the reader already believes | is it a description? if yes, it is not a take |

The body reveals in stages. Each section leaves something unresolved that the
next partially answers while opening a new question. The CTA closes the loop
**conditionally**: the reader reaches it knowing the shape of the answer and
needing the specific.

## Self-check before publishing
- [ ] S1 has a hard metric or a specific object
- [ ] S2 widens; no mechanism revealed
- [ ] S3 is an opinion, not a fact
- [ ] Reader cannot get the full payload without the CTA (if the goal is replies)
- [ ] One metaphor per post
