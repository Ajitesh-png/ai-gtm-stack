# Watchlist (example)

Copy to `context/watchlist.md`. Read by `content-scout`, `format-scout`,
`content-engagement-analyst` and the `content-routine` command as the set of
accounts whose public posts are worth studying for format and hook mechanics.

Two kinds of account, kept separate on purpose:

- **Format donors.** Big operator or creator accounts whose posts show what is
  working right now. Study their mechanics, never copy their copy.
- **Competitors.** Brand and founder accounts in your category. Study their
  content; never engage them as prospects (see competitor-skip-list).

| Handle | Platform | Kind | Why it's here | Added |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| @example_operator | X | format donor | 60k followers, posts real ad creative weekly, high bookmark ratio | 2026-01 |
| @example_competitor | X | competitor | direct competitor's brand account | 2026-01 |
| Example Name | LinkedIn | format donor | consistent carousel format, 300+ reactions per post | 2026-01 |

Verify every handle on the platform before adding it. Lookalike accounts with
copied bios are common; a handle with under ~500 followers or zero posts is
usually a decoy.
