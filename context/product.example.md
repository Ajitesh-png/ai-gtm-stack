# Product context (example)

Copy this file to `context/product.md` and describe **your** product. The radar
exposes it as the `radar://product` resource and the `icp_context` tool so the
`daily_brief` prompt can judge competitor posts against what you actually sell.

## What it is

An AI creative agent for performance marketers. Give it a direction ("30-second
UGC-style ad for the summer drop, Gen Z, use last shoot's B-roll") and it
researches hooks, writes the script, generates the avatar and voiceover, pulls
or generates B-roll, edits, and delivers a finished ad. Brand context (colors,
tone, product, approved hooks) is loaded once and remembered.

## Who buys it

Growth and paid-social teams whose bottleneck is creative volume: they know what
the ad should feel like and cannot produce variants fast enough to beat ad
fatigue.

## The argument against the market

Every other tool is a module (avatar tool, video model, template generator,
editor). The buyer is the glue between modules. The product replaces the buyer
as the agent, so the comparison that matters is "how much operating is left for
the human", not "whose clips look best".

## What a competitor post would have to say to matter

- Speaks to creative volume, fatigue, CAC/ROAS, testing cadence
- Uses the buyer's vocabulary (media buyer, DTC, UA, paid social)
- Teaches a portable move (framework, breakdown, before/after)

A model demo with a million views and none of the above is noise for this ICP.
