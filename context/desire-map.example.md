# Desire map (example)

Copy to `context/desire-map.md`. The `content-routine` command requires every
post angle to channel one named desire or fear from this map, which is what
stops the output drifting into "AI is cool" posts. Based on Eugene Schwartz's
observation that copy cannot create desire, only channel desire that already
exists.

Write it for **your** buyer. One line each: the desire, and the sentence they
would say out loud.

## Desires
| Name | What they want | How they'd say it |
|---|---|---|
| Leverage without headcount | Output of a team, payroll of one | "I can't hire my way out of this." |
| Being early | To have the edge before it's obvious | "Everyone will do this in a year." |
| Control of the loop | Seeing why something worked, not just that it did | "Show me the reasoning." |

## Fears
| Name | What they dread | How they'd say it |
|---|---|---|
| The dying window | The winning ad decays before the replacement exists | "My best ad has a week left." |
| Dependency | Freelancers, agencies, tools they cannot operate themselves | "I'm one contractor away from zero output." |
| Being passed | A smaller competitor out-shipping them | "How are they putting out 40 a week?" |

## Off-limits
Desires you will not exploit, and why (e.g. health anxiety, financial desperation).
